WAUKEE NORTHWEST BOYS’ BASKETBALL

Meet Coach Brett Watson

HEAD COACH SINCE 2021

Watson has led the Wolves since their inaugural season in 2021–22. Before coming to Northwest, he went 66–24 in four seasons at Indianola. His coaching stops also include ADM, Creston, Norwalk, and Minot State.

As a player, Watson scored 2,355 points at Elk Horn–Kimballton and earned first-team all-conference honors at Wayne State College in 2005. The IHSAA inducted him into its Basketball Hall of Fame in 2022.
